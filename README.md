# Onwei's Web Builder

A free website design studio that runs on your own computer. Design visually, then export clean HTML and CSS or publish straight to your own Cloudflare account.

No sign-in, no subscription, no Onwei server. A site is a folder on your PC, and everything you make is yours.

This repository only holds the installers. Download the latest from **[Releases](../../releases/latest)**.

## Install

1. Open the latest release and download the **Setup .exe** under Assets.
2. Run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Onwei opens with a short start-up guide.

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

## What it does

- **Design**: classes, combo classes and states; breakpoints; flex and grid; components; a section library.
- **Content**: a CMS with collections and a page made for each item; rich text; forms that email you (FormSubmit or Web3Forms).
- **Media**: images with smaller copies made for phones; video; Lottie animations; 3D models; maps.
- **Motion**: interactions and animations.
- **Site settings**: SEO, sitemap, fonts and custom code.
- **Check it**: stage the site on your computer, and open it on your phone with a QR code.
- **Export**: plain HTML and CSS you can put on any host.
- **Publish**: to your own Cloudflare account, free. Only changed files are sent, and earlier publishes can be put back. Connect your own domain, and password-protect pages, folders or the whole site.

## Free means free

Onwei costs nothing and holds nothing back. The only costs are ones you choose with other companies, such as a domain name.
